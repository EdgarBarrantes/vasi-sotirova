Commission a Painting

Vasilka Sotirova is a Bulgarian painter based in central Bulgaria, with more than thirty years of commissioned work in oil and acrylic. She paints portraits and landscapes, working from photographs you send or from life itself and her own inspiration. Every commission is an original, one-of-a-kind painting, signed and shipped worldwide.

What you can commission

  • Portraits

    Single, couple and family portraits in oil or acrylic, from a head-and-shoulders study to a full-length figure. Wedding and anniversary portraits are a specialty.

    See the portraits

  • Pet portraits

    Dogs, cats and horses painted from your photographs. A good, sharp photo in natural light is all that is needed.

    See the animal portraits

  • Landscapes and places

    A family house, a village street, a mountain or a stretch of the Black Sea coast, painted in Vasilka’s bold, high-key colour.

    See the landscapes

  • Still life and abstract work

    Flower paintings and abstract compositions to a size and palette that suits your room.

    See the abstract paintings

How it works

  1. Send your idea

    Email a few reference photographs, the subject, and the size you have in mind. If you are not sure about size or medium, say so and Vasilka will suggest what works best.

  2. Agree the details

    You will receive a reply with a proposed size, medium and price. Nothing is started until you approve.

  3. The painting is made

    You will see a photograph of the finished painting before it ships.

  4. Delivery

    Paintings are carefully packed and shipped worldwide from Bulgaria.

Prices

Prices depend on the size, the paints used and the number of figures, so every commission is quoted individually. Ask for a quote with no obligation.
