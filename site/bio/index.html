Bio

1965
Born in the city of Varna.
1984
Graduated from the High School of Applied Arts in the town of Tryavna.
1989
Graduated from the University of Fine Arts "Todor Samodumov" in the town of Dupnitsa.
2008
Postgraduate degree in "Pedagogy of the Plastic Arts".

Her paintings are owned by private individuals from Bulgaria, Germany, Russia, France, Norway, Sweden, Denmark, England, Canada, the USA and others.
